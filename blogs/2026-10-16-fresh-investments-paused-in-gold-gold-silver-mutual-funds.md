We would like to inform you that all mutual fund houses have temporarily stopped accepting fresh investments in their Gold and Gold-Silver Funds. This is effective from 3 PM on 15 Oct 2026 until further notice. What this means for you: New lump sum, SIPs, or step-ups in existing SIPs won’t be accepted Existing SIPs will continue as usual You can redeem or withdraw your investment at any time Orders received before 3 PM on 15 Oct will be processed
