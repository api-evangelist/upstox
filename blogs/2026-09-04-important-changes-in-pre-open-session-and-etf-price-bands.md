From 7 September 2026, there are a few changes to how orders are placed and matched during the pre-open session. Also, trading in Gold and Silver ETFs will now be allowed during the pre-open session. Other ETFs (including Equity, Debt, Liquid and Overnight ETFs) will not be available (same as current rules).
